# The Worker Character IP

## Character Definition

"The Worker" is the recurring visual character in all illustrations—not a mascot or sticker, but a serious participant in the system's operation doing absurd work.

## Appearance

**Overall form:** Simple human figure, stick-sketch style. Entirely solid black line art. MUST be clearly recognizable as human, never a blob.

**Body Structure (PARTS MUST BE VISIBLY SEPARATE):**

1. **Head**: One small circle or oval at the very top.
   - Inside: two white dot eyes, centered, blank stare.
   - No mouth, nose, or facial details.
   - Sits ON TOP, clearly separated from torso below.

2. **Torso**: One rectangle or narrow vertical oval directly below the head.
   - Attached to bottom of head.
   - Width: narrower than head.
   - Height: about 1.5× the head.
   - Outline only, no fill.

3. **Arms**: TWO SEPARATE thin lines extending horizontally from upper sides of torso.
   - Each arm is one thin line (stick arm), slightly bent or straight.
   - Each arm ends in a small circle (hand).
   - Left arm extends left, right arm extends right.
   - VISIBLY SEPARATE from torso, not merged into it.

4. **Legs**: TWO SEPARATE thin lines extending downward from bottom of torso.
   - Each leg is one thin line (stick leg).
   - Slightly splayed or together, dealer's choice.
   - VISIBLY SEPARATE from torso.

**Critical:** When squinting, silhouette = circle (head) + rectangle (body) + line (left arm) + line (right arm) + line (left leg) + line (right leg). GAPS visible between parts. Not a blob. Not a potato. Not abstract.

**Style**: Hand-drawn, slightly wobbly, imperfect lines. Not geometric, not symmetrical, not polished.

**Expression**: Blank, deadpan, serious. Achieved through white dot eyes and posture. Never cute, never rounded, never soft.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something strange.
- Like a quiet system operator or technician.
- Dry humor, never cutesy.
- A bit clumsy, but not incompetent.
- Like an actual crew member in a whiteboard sketch doing real work.

## Common Roles

Place the Worker in the core action:

- Moving/carrying materials.
- Pulling lines to connect information sources.
- Stuck at a bottleneck.
- Operating a decision lever inside a machine.
- Transforming into a filter or funnel.
- Separating or sorting elements.
- Stamping or approving a document.
- Guiding a connection path.
- Holding up a warning sign at a pitfall.
- Reaching out but not catching something.
- Beside the action: building, bridging, opening doors, sorting, recording.

## Taboos

- ❌ Don't make The Worker an overly cute mascot.
- ❌ Don't render as a children's cartoon character.
- ❌ Don't add complex clothing, emoji-like expressions, shiny eyes.
- ❌ Don't leave The Worker standing in a corner just watching.
- ❌ Don't let The Worker steal focus from the core structure.
- ❌ Don't make it too commercial, too round, too polished.

## Test Standard

If you remove The Worker and the core metaphor still works completely, The Worker is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so The Worker performs the essential action.
