# Visual Identity & Style DNA

## One-Sentence Summary

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, heavy white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, readable but not instructional.

Like a professional in design, engineering, or product who sketched a quick explanation on blank paper.

## Core Requirements

- **16:9 horizontal sketch illustration** for inline article content.
- **Pure white background**—no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, vintage effects.
- **Black hand-drawn line art**—fine lines, subtle wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not thick outlines.
- **Heavy white space**—main subject occupies ~40–60% of canvas; minimum 35% blank space; ideally one fully quiet area.
- **Sparse handwritten annotations**—max 5–8 labels, each 2–8 words.
- **One image, one core action/structure/state/metaphor.**
- **Natural structure expression**—don't label the structure type on the image.

## Color Palette

- **Black**: main line art, character, frames, structure, text, primary objects.
- **Red**: key warnings, problems, emotional points, critical alerts, results.
- **Orange**: primary flow, paths, arrows, automation flow, movement from A→B.
- **Blue**: secondary notes, mental state, system state, secondary explanation, AI/automation cues.

Blue is optional. Use color sparingly; less is better.

## Absolute Don'ts

- ❌ Commercial illustration.
- ❌ PPT infographic.
- ❌ Formal flowchart or diagram.
- ❌ Course slide or textbook.
- ❌ Cute cartoon or mascot poster.
- ❌ Children's illustration.
- ❌ Complex architecture diagram.
- ❌ Polished flat design.
- ❌ Sci-fi UI or corporate aesthetic.
- ❌ Real app screenshots.
- ❌ Complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- ❌ Over-explained nodes and connections.
- ❌ Top-left titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap."

## Aesthetic Direction

**Aim for:** strange, creative, engaging, clean, simple, imaginative.

**Avoid:** cute, childish, overly complex, rigid, dead.
